# Story: Multi-department

**Number**: 55
**Area**: Platform
**Priority**: P4

## What do we want?

**As a** Admin, **I want to** scope tickets/users to departments, **so that** different teams manage their own tickets.

## Context

This story is one of the Platform capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Admin cannot scope tickets/users to departments, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** an organization with departments, **When** a ticket is created, **Then** it is scoped to the correct department

## Why it matters

Different teams manage their own tickets. Priority P4 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Platform area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/55-multi-department/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
