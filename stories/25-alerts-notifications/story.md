# Story: Alerts and notifications

**Number**: 25
**Area**: SLA & Automation
**Priority**: P2

## What do we want?

**As a** Agent, **I want to** get alerted when an SLA deadline is approaching or breached, **so that** I can act before it's too late.

## Context

This story is one of the SLA & Automation capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Agent cannot get alerted when an SLA deadline is approaching or breached, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** an SLA deadline is approaching or breached, **When** the threshold is crossed, **Then** the responsible agent/supervisor is alerted

## Why it matters

I can act before it's too late. Priority P2 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the SLA & Automation area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/25-alerts-notifications/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
