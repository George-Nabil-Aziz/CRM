# Story: Track requests

**Number**: 36
**Area**: Customer Portal
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** track the status of my requests, **so that** I know progress without contacting support.

## Context

This story is one of the Customer Portal capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot track the status of my requests, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** an open ticket, **When** the customer views the portal, **Then** its current status is shown

## Why it matters

I know progress without contacting support. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Customer Portal area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/36-track-requests/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
