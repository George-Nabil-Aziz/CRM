# Story: Add notes and attachments

**Number**: 04
**Area**: Customer Management
**Priority**: P1

## What do we want?

**As a** Agent, **I want to** add a note or attachment to a customer profile, **so that** relevant details are preserved for future interactions.

## Context

This story is one of the Customer Management capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Agent cannot add a note or attachment to a customer profile, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a customer profile, **When** an agent adds a note or attachment, **Then** it is saved and visible to any agent who opens the profile later

## Why it matters

Relevant details are preserved for future interactions. Priority P1 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Customer Management area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/04-notes-attachments/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
