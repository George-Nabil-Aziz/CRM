# Story: Management dashboards

**Number**: 44
**Area**: Reports & Management
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Executive, **I want to** see key metrics at a glance, **so that** I get an overview without running individual reports.

## Context

This story is one of the Reports & Management capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Executive cannot see key metrics at a glance, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** an executive/manager, **When** they open the dashboard, **Then** key metrics across tickets/SLA/agents/CSAT are summarized at a glance

## Why it matters

I get an overview without running individual reports. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Reports & Management area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/44-management-dashboards/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
