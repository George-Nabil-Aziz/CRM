# Story: Custom branding

**Number**: 57
**Area**: Platform
**Priority**: P4

## What do we want?

**As a** Admin, **I want to** configure custom branding, **so that** the portal matches our company identity.

## Context

This story is one of the Platform capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Admin cannot configure custom branding, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** an admin has configured branding, **When** any user views the CRM/portal, **Then** the configured logo/colors are shown

## Why it matters

The portal matches our company identity. Priority P4 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Platform area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/57-custom-branding/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
