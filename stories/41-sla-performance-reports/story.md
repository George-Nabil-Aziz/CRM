# Story: SLA performance reports

**Number**: 41
**Area**: Reports & Management
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Manager, **I want to** view SLA compliance, **so that** I can track service targets.

## Context

This story is one of the Reports & Management capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Manager cannot view SLA compliance, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** SLA targets are configured, **When** a manager views the report, **Then** compliance rates per team/agent are shown

## Why it matters

I can track service targets. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Reports & Management area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/41-sla-performance-reports/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
