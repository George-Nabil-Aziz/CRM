# Story: View history

**Number**: 37
**Area**: Customer Portal
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** view my past ticket history, **so that** I can reference previous issues.

## Context

This story is one of the Customer Portal capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot view my past ticket history, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a customer with past tickets, **When** they open the portal, **Then** their full ticket history is listed

## Why it matters

I can reference previous issues. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Customer Portal area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/37-view-history-portal/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
