# Story: AI chatbot

**Number**: 34
**Area**: AI Features
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** get instant answers from a chatbot, **so that** I don't have to wait for a human agent.

## Context

This story is one of the AI Features capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot get instant answers from a chatbot, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a customer with a question, **When** they interact with the chatbot, **Then** they get an instant answer or are handed off to a human agent

## Why it matters

I don't have to wait for a human agent. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the AI Features area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/34-ai-chatbot/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
