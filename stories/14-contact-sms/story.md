# Story: Contact via SMS

**Number**: 14
**Area**: Communication Channels
**Priority**: P2

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** text support, **so that** I don't need an app or internet connection.

## Context

This story is one of the Communication Channels capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot text support, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a customer texts support, **When** the SMS is received, **Then** a ticket is created/updated from it

## Why it matters

I don't need an app or internet connection. Priority P2 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Communication Channels area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/14-contact-sms/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
