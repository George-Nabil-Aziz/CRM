# Story: Read help articles and guides

**Number**: 27
**Area**: Knowledge Base
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** read a help article or guide, **so that** I can resolve my issue myself.

## Context

This story is one of the Knowledge Base capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot read a help article or guide, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a customer or agent, **When** they open an article, **Then** the solution/guide content is displayed

## Why it matters

I can resolve my issue myself. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Knowledge Base area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/27-help-articles-guides/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
