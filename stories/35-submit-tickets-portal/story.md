# Story: Submit tickets via portal

**Number**: 35
**Area**: Customer Portal
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** submit a ticket through the portal, **so that** I don't need to email or call.

## Context

This story is one of the Customer Portal capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot submit a ticket through the portal, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a logged-in customer, **When** they submit a ticket through the portal, **Then** it is created and visible in their history

## Why it matters

I don't need to email or call. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Customer Portal area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/35-submit-tickets-portal/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
