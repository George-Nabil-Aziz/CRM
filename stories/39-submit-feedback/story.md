# Story: Submit feedback

**Number**: 39
**Area**: Customer Portal
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** submit feedback/rating after a ticket is resolved, **so that** I can express my satisfaction.

## Context

This story is one of the Customer Portal capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot submit feedback/rating after a ticket is resolved, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a resolved ticket, **When** the customer submits feedback/rating, **Then** it is recorded against that ticket

## Why it matters

I can express my satisfaction. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Customer Portal area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/39-submit-feedback/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
