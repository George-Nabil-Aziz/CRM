# Story: Access FAQs

**Number**: 38
**Area**: Customer Portal
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Customer, **I want to** browse/search FAQs from the portal, **so that** I can self-solve simple issues.

## Context

This story is one of the Customer Portal capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Customer cannot browse/search FAQs from the portal, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a customer in the portal, **When** they browse/search FAQs, **Then** relevant answers are shown

## Why it matters

I can self-solve simple issues. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Customer Portal area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/38-access-faqs-portal/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
