# Story: Ticket reports

**Number**: 40
**Area**: Reports & Management
**Priority**: P3

## What do we want?

**As a** Manager, **I want to** run a ticket volume/status report, **so that** I can monitor support operations.

## Context

This story is one of the Reports & Management capabilities from the source feature list. It matters because without it, a(n) Manager cannot run a ticket volume/status report, which blocks the value described above from being delivered.

## Example

Concretely: **Given** a date range, **When** a manager runs a ticket report, **Then** volume and status breakdowns are shown

## Why it matters

I can monitor support operations. Priority P3 reflects how central this is relative to the rest of the Reports & Management area and the product as a whole — see `../../specs/40-ticket-reports/spec.md` for the full technical specification of exactly what the system must do.
